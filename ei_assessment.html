📐 Scoring Method

Each of the 35 questions uses a 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree → 5 = Strongly Agree). Dimension scores are the sum of their 5 questions (max 25 each). The 10 EI-Effectiveness questions are added to the total (max 50). Total score = sum of all 35 questions = max 175.
