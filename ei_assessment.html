📐 Scoring Method

Each of the 35 questions is a behavioural or situational scenario with 4 options scored 1–4 based on EI alignment (4 = most EI-aligned, 1 = least). The 5 core dimensions (Q1–25) are worth max 20 each. The 10 EI-Effectiveness questions (Q26–35) are worth max 40. Total max = 140.

High EI

112–140

Consistently high EI across all dimensions. Ready for leadership roles and autonomous cross-functional work.

Moderate EI

Solid foundation with identifiable growth areas. Targeted coaching in weak dimensions is recommended.

Developing EI

35–76

Significant development needed. Structured EI training, mentoring, and peer feedback programs advised.
